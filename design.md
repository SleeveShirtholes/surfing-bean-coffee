# The Surfing Bean Coffee Company — Design Direction

## Brand Vibe
Pacific Northwest coastal surf — relaxed, warm, family-owned feel. Think crashing waves, sandy shores, Oregon mist, and a hot cup of coffee at sunrise.

## Typography
- **Display**: 'Pacifico' — for the logo/hero title (surfy, handwritten coastal feel)
- **Heading**: 'Playfair Display' — warm editorial serif for section titles
- **Body**: 'Poppins' — clean, modern, readable sans-serif

## Color Palette

```css
--white:         #FFFFFF

```

## Layout Principles
- Full-width sections with alternating sand/white/blue backgrounds
- Generous padding, not cramped
- Wave SVG dividers between sections
- Mascot logo prominently featured in hero
- Mobile-first responsive grid

## Motion
- Fade-in-up on scroll for content blocks
- Smooth scroll between sections
- Subtle wave animation in hero background
